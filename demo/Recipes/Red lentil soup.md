# Red lentil soup

## Ingredients

- 300 g [[Red lentils]], rinsed
- 2 [[Onions]], chopped
- 3 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 3 [[Carrots]], diced
- 1 tbsp [[Curry powder]]
- 1 tsp [[Ground cumin]]
- 1 can [[Chopped tomatoes]] (400 g)
- 1.5 l water
- 2 [[Vegetable stock cubes]]
- 1 [[Lemon]], juiced
- [[Salt]] and [[Black pepper]] to taste

## Method

1. Soften the onion, garlic and carrot in a splash of water for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the curry powder and cumin and cook for a minute.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes, water and stock cubes. Simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils fall apart.
4. Blend half the soup, stir it back in and finish with lemon juice, salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Freezes well. Make a double batch and keep half for a busy evening.
