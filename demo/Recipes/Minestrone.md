# Minestrone

## Ingredients

- 1 [[Onions]], chopped
- 3 [[Carrots]], diced
- 2 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 1 can [[Chopped tomatoes]] (400 g)
- 1 can [[Kidney beans]] (400 g), drained
- 150 g [[Pasta]]
- 200 g [[Frozen peas]]
- 1.5 l water
- 2 [[Vegetable stock cubes]]

## Method

1. Soften the onion, carrot and garlic for 5 minutes.
2. Add the tomatoes, water and stock and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Add the pasta, beans and peas and cook until the pasta is done.
