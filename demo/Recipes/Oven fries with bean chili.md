# Oven fries with bean chili

## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg [[Potatoes]], cut into wedges
- 1 tbsp [[Olive oil]]
- 1 tsp [[Smoked paprika]]
- 2 cans [[Kidney beans]] (400 g each), drained
- 1 can [[Corn]] (300 g), drained
- 1 [[Onions]], chopped
- 2 [[Bell peppers]], diced
- 1 can [[Chopped tomatoes]] (400 g)
- 1 tsp [[Ground cumin]]
- 1 tsp [[Chili flakes]]
- 1 [[Avocado]], sliced

## Method

1. Toss the potato wedges with the oil and paprika and roast at 220 °C for 35 minutes.
2. Fry the onion and pepper until soft, then add the beans, corn, tomatoes and spices.
3. Simmer the chili for 15 minutes while the fries finish.
4. Serve the chili over the fries with sliced avocado.
