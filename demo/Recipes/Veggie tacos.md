# Veggie tacos

## Ingredients

- 8 [[Tortillas]]
- 1 can [[Kidney beans]] (400 g), drained
- 1 can [[Corn]] (300 g), drained
- 1 [[Onions]], sliced
- 2 [[Bell peppers]], sliced
- 1 tsp [[Smoked paprika]]
- 1 tsp [[Ground cumin]]
- 2 [[Avocado]], mashed
- 1 [[Lime]], juiced
- 4 [[Tomatoes]], diced

## Method

1. Fry the onion and pepper with the spices until soft, then warm the beans and corn through.
2. Mash the avocado with lime juice and a pinch of salt.
3. Warm the tortillas and let everyone build their own.
