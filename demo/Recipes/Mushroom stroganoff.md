# Mushroom stroganoff

## Ingredients

- 500 g [[Mushrooms]], sliced
- 1 [[Onions]], chopped
- 2 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 1 tsp [[Smoked paprika]]
- 200 ml [[Oat milk]]
- 1 tbsp [[Soy sauce]]
- 400 g [[Pasta]]
- 1 handful [[Parsley]], chopped

## Method

1. Cook the pasta.
2. Fry the mushrooms hard until browned, then add the onion, garlic and paprika.
3. Pour in the oat milk and soy sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Serve over the pasta with parsley.
