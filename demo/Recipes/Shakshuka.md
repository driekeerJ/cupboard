# Shakshuka

## Ingredients

- 6 [[Eggs]]
- 2 cans [[Chopped tomatoes]] (400 g each)
- 1 [[Onions]], chopped
- 2 [[Bell peppers]], diced
- 3 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 1 tsp [[Smoked paprika]]
- 1 tsp [[Ground cumin]]
- 1 [[Bread]], for dipping

## Method

1. Soften the onion, pepper and garlic with the spices.
2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes until thick.
3. Make six wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook for 6 minutes.
4. Serve straight from the pan with bread.
