# Overnight oats

## Ingredients

- 100 g [[Rolled oats]]
- 250 ml [[Oat milk]]
- 1 [[Bananas]], mashed
- 1 tbsp [[Peanut butter]]
- 1 [[Apples]], grated

## Method

1. Stir everything together in a jar.
2. Leave in the fridge overnight. Eat cold or warm it up.
