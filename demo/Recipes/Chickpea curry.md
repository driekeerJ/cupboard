# Chickpea curry

## Ingredients

- 2 cans [[Chickpeas]] (400 g each), drained
- 1 can [[Coconut milk]] (400 ml)
- 1 can [[Chopped tomatoes]] (400 g)
- 2 [[Onions]], chopped
- 3 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 1 thumb [[Ginger]], grated
- 2 tbsp [[Curry powder]]
- 200 g [[Frozen spinach]]
- 300 g [[Rice]]
- 1 [[Lime]], cut in wedges

## Method

1. Cook the rice.
2. Soften the onion, garlic and ginger for 5 minutes, then stir in the curry powder.
3. Add the chickpeas, tomatoes and coconut milk. Simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Serve over rice with a wedge of lime.
