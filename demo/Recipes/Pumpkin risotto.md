# Pumpkin risotto

## Ingredients

- 350 g [[Arborio rice]]
- 1 [[Pumpkin]] (about 1 kg), peeled and cubed
- 1 [[Onions]], finely chopped
- 2 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 1.2 l water
- 2 [[Vegetable stock cubes]]
- 1 tbsp [[Olive oil]]
- [[Black pepper]] to taste

## Method

1. Roast half the pumpkin at 200 °C for 25 minutes; simmer the other half in the stock until soft and blend.
2. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil, add the rice and toast for a minute.
3. Add the pumpkin stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for about 20 minutes.
4. Fold in the roasted pumpkin and finish with pepper.
