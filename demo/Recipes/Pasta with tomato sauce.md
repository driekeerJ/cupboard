# Pasta with tomato sauce

## Ingredients

- 500 g [[Pasta]]
- 1 [[Onions]], finely chopped
- 2 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 500 g [[Passata]]
- 1 tsp [[Chili flakes]]
- 1 tbsp [[Olive oil]]
- 150 g [[Frozen spinach]]
- [[Salt]] and [[Black pepper]] to taste

## Method

1. Cook the pasta according to the packet.
2. Meanwhile fry the onion and garlic in the olive oil until soft.
3. Add the passata, chili flakes and spinach and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Toss the pasta through the sauce and season.
