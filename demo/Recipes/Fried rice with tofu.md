# Fried rice with tofu

## Ingredients

- 300 g [[Rice]], cooked and cooled
- 400 g [[Tofu]], cubed
- 2 tbsp [[Soy sauce]]
- 1 tbsp [[Sesame oil]]
- 3 cloves [[Garlic]], minced
- 1 thumb [[Ginger]], grated
- 2 [[Carrots]], diced
- 200 g [[Frozen peas]]
- 3 [[Eggs]], beaten
- 1 bunch [[Spring onions]], sliced

## Method

1. Fry the tofu in the sesame oil until golden and set aside.
2. Stir-fry the garlic, ginger and carrot for 3 minutes, add the peas.
3. Push everything aside, scramble the eggs in the empty half of the pan.
4. Add the rice and soy sauce, toss well, and finish with tofu and spring onions.

## Notes

- Day-old rice fries best. Cook it the evening before.
